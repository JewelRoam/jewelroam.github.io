We consider a spread-out display unacceptable. We hang the paintings close together, in three or sometimes four rows, but we construct the display so that the artworks celebrate each other, as if they were partying on the walls. Normally, we exclude mediocre things and pursue integrity of perception and the greatest possible expressivity. The display should also be exciting. Viewers should experience unexpected, rapturous sensations; they should be thrilled by the encounter with great art. This is what we think a museum should be...

I. Savitsky
